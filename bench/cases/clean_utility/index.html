Cold Store Opening hours

Whatever the boats brought in, cut while you decide what to do with it.

Two counters, one of them ice. We buy off four boats and stop buying when the slab is full, which is usually before eleven and sometimes before nine.

On the slab

Turbot when there is turbot, which there is not often. Otherwise plaice, hake, gurnard, and whatever the crab boat has decided to sell us rather than send south.

We fillet at the counter and we will not do it in advance, because a fillet that has sat an hour is a different fish from the one you were shown.

Since 1961

Four boats

The shop has changed hands twice and the ice machine three times. The slab is the original and will outlast all of us.

Hours

Tuesday to Saturday, from seven until the slab is bare.

Closed Monday. Closed whenever the boats stay in, which the board outside says.
